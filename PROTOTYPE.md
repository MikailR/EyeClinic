# Prototype notes

- **Model id:** grok-4.6

## Visual direction

Sparse blue and white, taken from the glass storefront: the bright clinic-blue wordmark, the almond eye mark, and the two-panel “wave” headers that already list tests on the left and treatments on the right. Plenty of white, system fonts, no stock-photo collage and no invented doctor portraits — initials sit in labelled placeholders until real photographs exist.

## Trinidad UX choices

Call and WhatsApp are first-class, not buried under a web-only booking funnel: the sticky mobile bar is Call + Book, WhatsApp lives in the header, and the appointment form ends by sending a prefilled message rather than pretending a slot is held. Phone number, both addresses, and “5.0 on Google” are visible as text. Hours are stated as patterns (Chaguanas denser weekdays/Saturday; Tacarigua heavier Monday/Wednesday) and marked “confirm when booking” instead of inventing a timetable. Copy is plain Trinidad English — “come in”, “whichever is easier”, “old reports” — sized for a mid-range Android thumb without pinch-zoom.
